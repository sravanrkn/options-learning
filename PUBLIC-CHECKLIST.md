# Public checklist

What gets copied from the private workbench into this repo, and what never does.

## Published here
- Curriculum index (lesson titles, objectives, status)
- Lesson notes (`pack.md` sources + the corrected final understanding)
- Video scripts and YouTube links
- Selected paper-trade examples, sanitized (ticker, strategy, dates, outcome — no account values)
- Quarterly review summaries (lessons learned, not account numbers)

## Never published here
- Real-money trades, positions, or balances
- Account sizes, broker account IDs, API keys, broker config
- Raw quiz attempts and explain-back drafts
- Anything identifying a brokerage account

*Everything here is educational only, not financial advice.*
